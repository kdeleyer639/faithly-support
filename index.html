Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Faithly's core features do not require creating an account or signing in.

Can I read the Bible offline?

Faithly includes locally bundled Bible text and devotional content for offline reading.

How do I add the home screen widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your iPhone Home Screen, choose the option to add a widget, search for Faithly, and add the available widget. If it does not appear, open Faithly once and try again.

Why isn't the widget updating?

Open Faithly to refresh its content and confirm the widget theme in Settings. iOS may take some time to refresh home screen widgets.

How do I enable daily reminders?

Open Faithly's Settings to configure a reminder, and allow notifications when iOS asks. You can also adjust notification permission in iPhone Settings.

How do I track Bible reading?

Open a Bible chapter and mark it as read. Your chapter completion progress and Continue Reading section are shown within the app.

What if the app isn't working correctly?

Close and reopen the app, check that iOS and Faithly are updated, and restart your iPhone if needed. Contact support if the issue continues. Avoid deleting the app as locally saved content may be lost.
