Privacy Policy

Faithly – Grow in Faith
Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy describes how Faithly handles information when you use the mobile application. Please contact us if you have questions.

Information you provide

Faithly does not require an account or sign-in to use its core features. You may choose to save favorites, reading progress, reflections, and preferences within the app. These are intended to be stored on your device.

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and details of your request, so we can respond.

App data stored on your device

Faithly uses local device storage for app settings and features such as favorites, Bible reading progress, reading history, devotional interactions, and personal reflections. Removing the app or clearing its data may erase this information. Faithly does not currently offer an account-based backup or sync feature.

Notifications and widgets

If you enable daily reminders, Faithly requests notification permission and uses the settings you choose to schedule reminders. You can disable notifications through the app or your device settings. Faithly's home screen widget displays Bible verse content and may use locally shared app settings to match your selected appearance.

Internet connections and service providers

Core Bible reading and devotional content is designed to be available offline. The app may still communicate with platform services involved in app delivery, updates, or operating-system features. We do not intentionally sell personal information or use your personal reflections for advertising.

Information processed independently by Apple or other platform providers is subject to their respective privacy policies.

Sharing

If you choose to share a Bible verse using your device's sharing options, the content you select is sent to the destination you choose. Faithly does not control the privacy practices of those destinations.

Children's privacy

Faithly is designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through an account system. If you have a concern about information shared with support, contact us.

Data retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored Faithly data by deleting the app from your device, subject to any backups managed by your device or Apple. For information you sent directly to our support email, you can request deletion by contacting us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when updates are published.
